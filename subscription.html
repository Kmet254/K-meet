How Kmeet Chat Works

  • Sender: One opening “Hi” or “Hey” message is free.
  • Sender: After the free opening message, paid chat credits are required.
  • Receiver: The first 3 responses can be sent free.
  • Contact information: A receiver cannot reveal contact information without paying the KSh 11 continuation fee.
  • If a contact-revealing message is attempted: The message is declined/blocked and the receiver must pay KSh 11 to continue.
  • KSh 11: This is a continuation fee for the receiver, not a reward.
